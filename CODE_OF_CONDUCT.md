# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Disagree about the work, not about each
other.

## What is expected

- Keep discussion on the technical question. Reviews are about code.
- Accept that people have different levels of context. A question that seems
  obvious to you is not obvious to everyone, and answering it is cheaper than
  making someone feel stupid for asking.
- Take correction as well as you give it. Being wrong in public is part of
  building something correct.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning or discriminatory remarks, sustained
disruption, or publishing anyone's private information. Sexualised language and
imagery are not welcome anywhere in the project.

## A rule specific to this project

exav is a malware scanner, so contributors handle live malicious samples.

- **Never attach a live sample** to an issue, a pull request, or any other
  public channel. Give a SHA-256 and where it came from.
- **Never share a working exploit or amplification reproducer** for an unfixed
  bug in exav or in one of its dependencies until the fix has shipped.

This is not a formality. Publishing a sample or a reproducer hands a working
weapon to anyone reading, and a public repository is read by people who are not
here to help.

## Reporting

Raise it with the maintainer directly: <https://github.com/sylvinus>. Reports
are handled privately.

Consequences are proportionate, and range from a request to stop, through
temporary exclusion from a discussion, to a permanent ban. The maintainer
decides, and will say why.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.
